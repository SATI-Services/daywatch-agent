`## Next up` in `status/<author>.md` is your ordered backlog — top item = do-next, the single deterministic answer to "what should I work on next". Work enters it two ways: you add it, or a teammate/lead drops a note addressed to you and you triage it into `## Next up`. So the inbox (`notes/`) is newly-assigned-not-yet-triaged; `## Next up` is your owned queue.

**Why:** It is what lets the lead reconcile the whole team's queue without asking.
