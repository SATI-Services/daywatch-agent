`## Next up` in `status/<author>.md` is the author's owned, ordered backlog — top item = do-next, the single deterministic answer to "what should I work on next", and what lets the lead reconcile the whole team's queue without asking. Work enters it two ways: you add it, or a teammate/lead drops a note addressed to you and you triage it into `## Next up`. The `notes/` inbox is newly-assigned-not-yet-triaged; `## Next up` is the owned queue.

**Why:** Teammates can't edit your status file (write-partition), so the note-to-`## Next up` triage is the only path for assigned work to become scheduled.
